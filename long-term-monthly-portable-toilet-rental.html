Placement & Delivery Tips

For a placement that's genuinely going to sit for months, take a little extra time on positioning up front — firm, well-drained ground matters more over a long placement than a short one, since a soft spot that seems fine in week one can shift by week eight. Keep the surrounding area reasonably clear of equipment or overgrowth so our service truck has consistent access on every scheduled visit; a route that's blocked even occasionally tends to throw off the whole rotation. If your property has seasonal weather concerns — standing water during Florida's wet season, for example — mention it when we're choosing a spot, since we can often avoid the low-lying part of a lot entirely. And if you know your usage will shift significantly at some point (a farm's harvest season, a project's final push), give us as much advance notice as you can so we can plan the adjustment into our regular routing rather than scrambling to fit it in.
