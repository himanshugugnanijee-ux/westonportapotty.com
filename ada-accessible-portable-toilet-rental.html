Most Florida counties, including Broward, require at least one wheelchair-accessible restroom for any permitted public event above a certain attendance threshold — and beyond the permit checkbox, it's simply the right way to plan an event or job site where people of all mobility levels will be present. Our ADA-compliant units are built specifically to meet that need: a significantly wider door opening than a standard unit, a spacious interior floor plan sized for wheelchair turning radius, and securely mounted grab bars positioned near the toilet for safe transfer and support.

These units function like our deluxe restrooms in most other respects — foot-pump flush, sink access, interior lighting — but the structural differences are what make them compliant rather than just "bigger." Door swing, threshold height, interior clearance and grab bar placement all follow accessibility guidelines, so if your event has a permit requirement attached, this is the unit that satisfies it without a second thought.

Placement & Delivery Tips

Placement matters more for ADA units than any other type we offer — the unit needs a firm, level approach path free of gravel, sand or steps, so pavement, compacted gravel or a temporary matted path works best. Position it along the same accessible route as any ramps or accessible parking you've already planned, ideally within a short, obstacle-free distance from the main gathering or work area. If your event includes an accessible parking zone, placing the unit nearby rather than at the far end of the venue meaningfully improves usability, not just optics. For permitted public events, keep a copy of the unit specifications we provide on hand in case an inspector asks to confirm compliance on-site. And if you're not sure whether your specific venue layout qualifies as compliant placement, send us a quick site photo or description before your event — we'd rather flag a fix in advance than have it come up as a problem on the day itself.
