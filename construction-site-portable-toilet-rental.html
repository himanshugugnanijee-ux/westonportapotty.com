Placement & Delivery Tips

On active job sites, place units near the actual work zones rather than only at the site entrance — crews are far more likely to use a restroom that doesn't cost them a five-minute walk, which matters for both productivity and keeping other areas of the site clean. If your project moves through distinct phases with shifting crew locations, tell us that during setup so we can plan a relocation schedule alongside your regular servicing instead of treating each move as a one-off request. Fenced sites with restricted access should flag any gate codes or sign-in procedures in advance so delivery and weekly service visits aren't delayed at the entrance. For sites tracking safety or compliance documentation, we can provide servicing records on request — useful for larger contractors managing OSHA documentation across a project.
